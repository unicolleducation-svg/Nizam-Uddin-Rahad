PROFESSIONAL PHILOSOPHY

A person-centred approach to healthcare

Rahad’s professional background brings together formal medical assistant education, supervised clinical placements, hospital employment and community welfare coordination. These complementary experiences provide a perspective on healthcare that extends beyond clinical procedures to the everyday needs of patients and their families.

Patient-centred support involves respecting dignity, privacy and individual preferences while helping people maintain comfort and independence. In a healthcare assistant role, careful observation, accurate communication and timely escalation of concerns are particularly important. His training and hospital experience provide a foundation for contributing to these activities within the responsibilities assigned by supervising professionals.

Professional direction

His international career provides a basis for adapting to different healthcare environments and working with people from diverse backgrounds. In Portugal, his community welfare experience complements his clinical background, while continued Portuguese language development supports integration into local professional settings.
